## Who is responsible

This site belongs to Oskar Pajka, a motion designer and developer based in Kielce, Poland. I am a private person, not a company, and I am the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach me at oskar@oskarpajka.com about anything in this policy.

## What this site collects

Very little. The site has no accounts, no contact form, no comments, no newsletter, no shop and no ads. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device: no localStorage, no sessionStorage. The video player, the image comparison, the large image view and the button that copies my email address all run only in your browser and send nothing anywhere.

When your browser asks for a page, an image or a film, the request necessarily includes some technical data: your IP address, the address of the page, the time, your browser and system (the user agent) and the page you came from (the referrer). This data is used to deliver the site and to protect it from attacks. I do not receive lists of visitors or their IP addresses. I only see totals, such as the number of visits per page.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Visit statistics

To see which pages are read, I use Cloudflare Web Analytics. A small script from Cloudflare (loaded from static.cloudflareinsights.com) reports each page view: the page, the referrer, the browser and device type, the country and how fast the page loaded. It does not use cookies or any other storage on your device, and Cloudflare states that it does not fingerprint visitors or follow them across websites. Web addresses are recorded without query strings.

Cloudflare keeps the detailed data for 7 days and then keeps a reduced, aggregated sample. I can see up to six months of totals. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can block the script with any content blocker; the site works the same without it.

## Hosting and video

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Workers, and the films and large images are served from Cloudflare R2 at media.oskarpajka.com. The provider is Cloudflare, Inc., which processes the request data described above on my behalf as a processor, under its Data Processing Addendum. Cloudflare also uses some traffic data, such as threat scores, to keep its own network secure, as explained in its privacy policy (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy).

Cloudflare runs a global network, so your data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Cloudflare, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and its Data Processing Addendum includes the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses as a fallback.

The fonts are stored on this site. No requests go to Google Fonts, YouTube, Vimeo or social networks while you browse.

## Email

If you write to oskar@oskarpajka.com, I receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. I use it only to reply and to continue our conversation, for example about a project or a job. My email is hosted by Apple (iCloud Mail), which stores the messages for me.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering messages, or the steps you ask me to take before we agree on work together (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). I keep the conversation for as long as it is useful and delete it at the latest two years after our last message, unless a contract or the law requires me to keep it longer. I do not add you to any list and never share your message with anyone else.
